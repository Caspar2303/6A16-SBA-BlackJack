📜 Game Rules

  • Goal: Get a card total closer to 21 than the dealer without going over 21.
  • Card Values:
    • 2-10: Face value
    • J, Q, K: 10 points
    • Ace: 1 or 11 points
  • Actions:
    • Hit: Take another card.
    • Stand: Keep current hand and end turn.
    • Split: If initial two cards match in rank, split them into two separate hands.
  • Dealer Rule: Dealer must hit until cards total 17 or higher.

Minors are strictly prohibited from participating in gambling activities.
